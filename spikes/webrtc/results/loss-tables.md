## Uplink: browser to endpoint (the endpoint drops, then counts what it could still have)

"Dropped": frames whose packet the endpoint threw away. "Deep FEC": the LBRR inside a redundant block, which is the frame two back. "Skipped by the browser": holes in its RTP times that are not our drops. A run is marked "disturbed" when the browser skipped more than 1% of its frames or the endpoint itself sent more than 8 frames over 45 ms after the one before: the machine was busy with something else and the run says little.
